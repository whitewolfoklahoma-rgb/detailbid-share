How DetailBid works

Two ways to price the same job

Package mode prices from your package table: pick the vehicle class and the package, and DetailBid uses the price and hours you set for that combination. Time mode builds the job service by service: each service has a sedan base time, the vehicle-class factor scales it (a large SUV at 1.45× takes 45% longer than a sedan), and the total hours are priced at your shop rate. Both modes then add the same condition surcharges, add-ons, and mobile fee — and both show you the hours and the effective $/hour, because a $400 detail that takes 9 hours ($44/hr) is a worse job than a $300 detail that takes 4 ($75/hr).

Condition surcharges — say it before you see it

Pet hair is the famous one: it can add an hour or more of extraction and lint-rolling to any interior. Price it as a named surcharge on the quote — with your price and your extra time — instead of eating it or springing it on the customer at pickup. Heavy soil, odor, and stain work work the same way. Surcharges are flat dollars in both pricing modes, and their extra hours count in your time estimate and your $/hr.

The profit floor

Every quote is costed honestly: labor (estimated hours × your labor cost rate) + chemicals & supplies (your % of labor) + travel (mobile miles × your per-mile rate) = break-even. Your floor is the lowest price that still leaves your minimum margin — break-even ÷ (1 − margin) — and never less than your minimum job charge. Type a final price below the floor and DetailBid flags it and will not save the quote unless you tick the one-time override box and own the decision. Loading a saved quote to edit it resets the override, so an exception can't become a habit.

Mobile fee

Mobile fee = round-trip miles × your per-mile rate, with a minimum (a 4-mile trip still costs you setup and drive time). The same miles also feed the travel side of your break-even at the same rate, so a long drive raises both the price and the floor.

Your data

Everything is stored only in this browser (localStorage) on this device — no account, no cloud, no tracking, works fully offline. Export a backup from Settings & Backup before switching devices. Clearing browser data erases the app — export first.

Defaults to localize

The built-in package prices, service times, surcharges, and rates are national planning defaults — a starting point, not your market. Time your next three details against the estimate, adjust the tables in Settings, and re-check after ten jobs. A calculator is only as good as its inputs; this one makes yours easy to fix.
